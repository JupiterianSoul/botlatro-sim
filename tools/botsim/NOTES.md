## Layout

- `assets/bot/sim/rng.lua`: `pseudohash`, `pseudoseed`, `pseudorandom`, element picks and `pseudoshuffle`, working on the state's own `rng` array. RNG keys are interned to integer slots (`rng.key_id`), `pseudohash(key..seed)` is cached per seed and shared by clones. The `%.13f` rounding is kept as is.
- `assets/bot/sim/data.lua`, `data_jokers.lua`: hands, ranks, suits, enhancements, editions, seals, blinds, tags, vouchers, decks. `data_jokers.lua` is generated from `P_CENTERS` in `assets/game.lua` (joker id = its `order`).
- `assets/bot/sim/state.lua`: state layout (scalars plus flat numeric arrays, cards as struct of arrays indexed by card id), `clone`, `copy_into` (no allocation, for search buffers), `scratch`.
- `assets/bot/sim/eval.lua`: poker hand detection, a direct port of `evaluate_poker_hand`, `get_flush`, `get_straight`, `get_X_same`, `get_highest` on rank counts, with the game's quirks (Shortcut gap rules, Four Fingers, Smeared, Wild, Stone, Splash, five of a kind counting as four and three of a kind).
- `assets/bot/sim/econ.lua`: shop, packs, consumables, vouchers, tags, selling, rerolls (installed into the engine at load). `create_card`, `get_current_pool`, `poll_edition`, `get_pack`, `create_card_for_shop` ported with the game\'s RNG keys. `G.GAME.used_jokers` is derived on demand from the cards that exist (jokers, consumables, shop row, pack, plus the consumable being used).
- `assets/bot/sim/data_centers.lua`, `data_challenges.lua`: generated by `tools/botsim/gen/centers.lua` and `gen/challenges.lua` from `assets/game.lua` and `assets/challenges.lua`.
- `assets/bot/sim/engine.lua`: run start, blind select, skip, boss reroll, draw, play, discard, scoring pipeline, end of round, round money, cash out, leave shop. `E.step(s, action)` takes SPEC actions.
- `assets/bot/sim/snapshot.lua`, `json.lua`: SPEC snapshot line, small JSON encoder and decoder.
- `tools/botsim/bench.lua`, `replay.lua`, `policy.lua` (greedy policy used by the bench), `fuzz.lua` (random legal and illegal actions with random jokers, enhancements, seals and editions on every deck and stake, checks clones against the original), `test/run.lua`.

Run everything from the repo root:

```
nice -n 19 taskset -c 0-3 luajit tools/botsim/test/run.lua
nice -n 19 taskset -c 0-3 luajit tools/botsim/bench.lua 40
nice -n 19 taskset -c 0-3 luajit tools/botsim/replay.lua log.jsonl out.jsonl
nice -n 19 taskset -c 0-3 luajit tools/botsim/fuzz.lua 3000
```

## How the truth was checked

- The RNG tests load the game's own `pseudohash`, `pseudoseed`, `pseudorandom`, `pseudorandom_element`, `pseudoshuffle`, `get_new_boss`, `get_current_pool`, `get_next_tag_key` and `get_next_voucher_key` straight out of `assets/functions/*.lua` with a fake `G`, and compare: pseudohash on 20k random strings, 3000 mixed `pseudoseed` calls per seed, shuffles, the starting deck order, 20 antes of boss picks, tag and voucher picks.
- Scoring tests are hand derived from `evaluate_play`, `eval_card` and `Card:calculate_joker` (about 100 cases: jokers, editions, enhancements, seals, retriggers, held cards, bosses), plus boss blind setup tests through the real select flow and full greedy runs on every deck at stakes 1 and 8.

## Exact vs approximate

Exact (ported line by line, RNG keys mirrored, checked on CI against the real game):

- Card creating jokers and seals go through `create_card` at the time the game's events would run them: 8 Ball and Purple seal, Vagabond, Superposition, Seance, Sixth Sense, Cartomancer, Hallucination, Riff-raff, Blue seal, Perkeo, plus Throwback, Swashbuckler, Stencil and the tally fields shown in `ab`.
- Tags: all 24, including Double Tag copies, Orbital Tag hand (the three orbital rolls the blind select screen makes per ante), pack tags opening at blind select, Boss, Juggle, Voucher, Coupon, Uncommon and Rare (with the game's V2.130 fix), edition tags, Top-up.
- Challenges: rules, starting jokers (pinned jokers stay leftmost), consumables, vouchers, custom decks, banned cards, tags and blinds, no rewards, Luxury Tax hand size, Double or Nothing debuffs, Rich get Richer chip cap, Typecast, X-ray flipped cards, inflation, discard cost.
- Win phase (`win`) and endless.
- At game over the queued events of the end of round never run (no rental charge, no Popcorn or Ice Cream destruction); the sim does the same.

Known gaps (not seen in the data yet, or approximate):

- Wins and endless are implemented but CI has no recorded win yet; the recorder's mixed policy dies before ante 9.
- `chips_dollar_cap` is applied once before Plasma (exact for additive chips, which is all vanilla has).
- Luxury Tax: hand size is re-polled at the end of each action and before draws (the game polls every frame).
- Showman with a sold duplicate keeps `used_jokers` slightly differently (the game clears the key even if another copy sits in the shop).
- Hand sort by suit is not modelled (default rank sort only).
- Stone cards: the game gives them a fresh random negative id on every `get_id` call; the sim uses -1. Only the unseeded `math.random` state differs, and that state is reseeded before every seeded call.

## Rules this repo changes, and assumptions

- Profile state: the game skips locked vouchers and undiscovered tag requirements (`tag_rare` needs Blueprint discovered, edition tags need the edition). The sim assumes a fully unlocked profile.
- Overhaul additions ignored: creative stakes 9 to 12, Badge Protection (an equipped profile badge disables the matching boss), multiplayer lives and Nemesis, `first_used_hand_level`, the 10000 evaluation cap in `evaluate_play`. Mr. Bones only saves outside multiplayer in this repo; the sim is single player.
- `bl_mp_nemesis` is banned in single player and `bl_empty` has min ante 99, so the boss pool is vanilla.
- The first shop Buffoon Pack and the Charm and Meteor Tag packs picked their art variant with unseeded `math.random(1, 2)`; the game now uses the seeded keys `first_buffoon`, `charm_tag` and `meteor_tag` (FIX in `assets/DEV_CHANGELOG.txt`), and so does the sim.

- `pairs(G.GAME.hands)` order: fixed in the game (commit f466432), lists are sorted by hand order; the sim follows that.

## Snapshot choices the recorder must match

- `phase`: `blind_select`, `play`, `round_eval`, `shop`, `pack`, `win`, `game_over`.
- `blind` only while a blind is active.
- `ab` keys: ability fields by their name (`mult`, `x_mult`, `invis_rounds`, `yorick_discards`, `caino_xmult`, `loyalty_remaining`, `extra_value`), `ability.extra` as `extra` when it is a number, fields of `ability.extra` as `extra.<name>` (`extra.chips`, `extra.dollars`, `extra.h_size`).
- `levels` lists all 12 hands.
