### Changed (on main, d6ba411)
- `quick_clear=1`, a new option of the net's MCTS: in a play state, if the best visible play clears the blind, play it. The play is scored on `F.sanitize(state)` (canonical seed, hidden cards replaced, the same sanitizing Fair Seer uses), so it reads nothing hidden. Off by default; the full profile's imitation gate now evaluates with it (`imitation.eval_args`, passed to the eval and the shuffle check on CI and on Hetzner).
- `train.py --all-discards N` trains play records against every discard of the hand. Not used by the full profile (it did not beat the baseline, see below).
- MCTS `type_mass` / `tm_plays`: decide play or discard by summed probability. Only useful for all-discards nets.
- Tools: `train/round_stats.lua` (per-round D/P sequences, discards made while a play clears, for the net or Fair Seer), `train/vote_stats.py` (how teacher votes split).
